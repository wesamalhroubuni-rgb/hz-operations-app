# Privacy policy

**[App name]**, operated by **[Company legal name]** ("we"). Last updated: **[date]**.

[App name] is a workplace app. Your employer (the "organization") uses it so that its workers can
record their work and its supervisors and managers can review it. The organization decides who
has an account and what they can see. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: your name, and the email address, phone number or username your
  organization registered for you; your role and the sites and teams you belong to.
- **Work records**: the work logs and task updates you create, with their date and time, notes,
  and the details your organization asks for.
- **Location**: when you save a work log while the app is open, its position (if you allowed
  location). The app never tracks your location in the background.
- **Photos**: photos you take or choose for a work log. Location data embedded in a photo (EXIF) is
  removed when it is stored.
- **Voice notes**: if your organization turned on smart capture and you use "Speak", the recording
  is used to propose a work log for you to check. Nothing is saved as a work log until you save it.
- **Device information**: a notification token (to send you notifications), the app version, and
  sign-in sessions (device name, times).

## How we use it

Only to run the service for your organization: to show you your tasks, to store and send your
work records to the people in your organization who may see them, to send you notifications, and
to keep the service secure (for example, an audit log of changes).

When your organization turned on the intelligent features, your notes, voice notes and photos
are sent to an AI provider (**[Anthropic, and OpenAI for voice notes]**) to propose a work log or
to write a daily summary for supervisors. The AI never saves or changes anything by itself, and
names, contact details and identifiers are not sent to it.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

People in your organization according to their role and scope (for example, the supervisor of
your site). Our staff only when needed to operate or support the service. Service providers that
host the service for us (servers, storage, notifications, AI processing) under contract.

## Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit (https). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Work records are
kept for as long as your organization keeps its account, or as the law requires. Data waiting on
your phone to be sent is deleted after it is sent, or after 30 days at the latest.

## Your choices and rights

You can turn off location, camera, microphone and notification access in your phone settings at
any time; the app keeps working without them. To see, correct or delete your data, contact your
organization's administrator, or us at **[contact email]**.
